Popup Box2D Playground

A tiny physics toy where every body is a small popup window. Grant popup permission, then drag, shove, and bounce the windows around your screen.

Click start. Your browser should ask whether this page may open popups.

How to play

  • Drag a popup by holding inside it. Release to throw it.
  • Click a popup to punch it with a random impulse.
  • Use Kick all windows if they settle down.
  • If motion stutters while dragging, that is normal: browsers throttle cross-window moves.
